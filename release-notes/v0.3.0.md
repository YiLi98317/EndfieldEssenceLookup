# v0.3.0 — Weapon browser

This release rebuilds essence lookup into a side-by-side weapon browser with official icons, search, and the expanded weapon list.

## Downloads

- **macOS**: Download the `.dmg`
- **Windows**: Download the `.exe`

## Changes since v0.2.2

- **Weapon browser**: pick a weapon from a grid of official icons, search by name, and filter by rarity or type
- Selecting a weapon shows its icon, type, rarity, description, and essence stats next to the farming locations (the two panes stack on a narrow screen)
- The catalog grows from 68 to 80 weapons
- New window icon and a custom pointer cursor; the Chinese app title is 终末地小助手

## Notes

- 12 weapons have no essence stats yet, including 落草 and 仰止, which previously did. Those show that essence data has not been added yet. 应急手段 is no longer in the catalog.
- In English, weapon names and types still appear in Chinese.
- macOS builds are **not signed/notarized** yet (Gatekeeper may warn on first open; use right-click → Open).
